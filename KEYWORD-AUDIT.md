## Cannibalization: roof replacement cost

The live Nashville SERP for "roof replacement cost nashville" is **informational**: Reddit, then cost guides (mrgoodroof, qualityexteriors, This Old House, dominionx). No service page ranks. National demand is large: "roof replacement cost" gets 18,100 at KD 25, and "average cost of roof replacement" gets 6,600 at KD 9.

Recommendation: **one cost page, not three.**
- Merge the /pricing draft and the cost blog post into **one guide**. I'd keep the blog-post URL (`/blog/how-much-does-roof-replacement-cost-nashville`), because it matches how the pages that rank are built. /pricing can become a short nav link to it or be dropped.
- The roof-replacement service page links to the guide and stops targeting cost terms.
- The price range still has to be settled first. Competitors publish $15k–$30k (dominionx) and $8.5k–$24.5k statewide (qualityexteriors). The live site says $15k–$30k.
