## The angle

Stellar starts with **effectively zero real links**. Of the ~65 linking domains, 64 are automated spam "SEO checker" directories that appeared this month, and one is a real listing on n49.com. Google generally ignores the spam, so no action is needed on it.

The fastest wins are:
1. **Consistent business listings (citations)**, which feed Maps rankings.
2. **Local memberships and sponsorships**: chambers, a property-manager association, youth sports, Habitat for Humanity. These are the real local links competitors have that Stellar doesn't.

Work the "Do first" rows below before anything else.

## Why these

- **Chambers (7, 8):** these are the strongest real local links a contractor can get. The listings also feed Google local signals, and Hendersonville's leads exchanges can bring jobs directly.
- **Habitat Critical Repair (9):** roofing is exactly what that program needs. One sponsored or donated repair can bring a partner-page link, social posts, and a local news angle, and it gives the team real photos for the Business Profile.
- **Little League (10):** proven by a competitor. Don Kennedy's sponsorship link appears on 118 pages of one league site. Goodlettsville's league is 2 miles from your address.
- **CAI and GNAA (11, 12):** both link to National Roof Co, the business that ranks #1 in Maps near you. They also connect Stellar with the people who approve roof work for HOAs and apartment complexes, which fits the commercial roofing page.
- **Directories linked from 2+ competitors (16, 17):** if several local roofers get links from a site, Stellar usually can too.
