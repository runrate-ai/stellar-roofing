## The one fix: reviews

The profile has **zero Google reviews**. Every competitor in the map box near Goodlettsville and Nashville has 39–956 reviews at 4.7–5.0. Reviews are the biggest thing Stellar controls that decides map ranking, and they are also what homeowners read before calling. Nothing else on this list moves rankings as much until there are real reviews.

- Ask every past customer, starting with the most recent jobs, by text with the direct review link. The link is on the profile under "Ask for reviews". GoHighLevel can automate a review request after each job closes.
- Target: **20+ reviews in the next 60 days**, then a steady 4–6 a month. That's the pace National Roof Co keeps.
- **Reply to every review.** In the 20 most recent National Roof Co reviews, the owner replied to none. Replying is an easy way to stand out.
- Never gate reviews (asking only happy customers), pay or discount for them, or post them yourselves. Google removes them and can suspend the profile. The same real-reviews-only rule applies to the website.

## About "open 24 hours"

Among the top 10 map results for "roofing company nashville", 5 list 24-hour hours, including Don Kennedy (#3), Southern Roofing Co. (#5), and Henry & Sons (#6). Roofing searches spike at night and after storms, and Google favors businesses that are open when the search happens. **Only list 24 hours if calls are actually answered 24/7**, for example by an answering service or GoHighLevel's after-hours/AI call answering that books the job. Listing hours you don't keep breaks Google's guidelines and can get the profile suspended. A safe middle step is longer real hours, such as 7 AM–8 PM, seven days a week.

## What to do next

1. **Start collecting reviews now:** ask past customers and set up the post-job review text in GHL. Reply to every review.
2. **Upload 20+ real photos:** finished roofs (before/after), crew at work, trucks with branding, the team, and a cover photo. Keep adding a few after every job.
3. **Rewrite the description** (up to 750 characters): services, the Middle Tennessee service area, free inspections, insurance claim help, the lifetime workmanship warranty. No keyword stuffing, no links.
4. **Add every true secondary category and service.** Match the site's six services (replacement, repair, storm damage repair, inspection, commercial roofing, emergency repair) plus gutters or siding only if Stellar actually does them. Candidate categories to check in the profile editor: Construction company, General contractor, Gutter service, Water damage restoration service. Only add what's real.
5. **Fix hours:** choose real hours (24h only with 24/7 call answering) and update the profile and site schema together.
6. **Point the profile's website link** to `https://www.thestellarroofing.com/nashville?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp` so GA4 can tell profile visits apart.
7. **Align site schema** to the Goodlettsville address, coordinates, and real hours (small code change in `lib/schema.js`; needs your OK).
8. **Set the profile's service areas** to the cities the site serves (Nashville, Goodlettsville, Hendersonville, Gallatin, Madison, Franklin, Brentwood, Murfreesboro, and so on) if not already set.
9. **Start weekly posts** once 1–4 are done: recent jobs, storm alerts, free inspection offers.
10. **Re-run this grid** in 60 days to measure progress (~9 searches).
