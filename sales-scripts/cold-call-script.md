# Stellar cold-call script (from Nate's door-knocking pitch)

Built from what Nate said on the 2026-10-22 call. Say it in your own words, but keep the order: permission opener, reason for the call, qualify, book on the calendar.

**Goal of every call:** find homeowners who (1) know they need a roof and (2) are actively looking. Three good appointments beat ten okay ones.

---

## 1. Opener: "good news, bad news"

> **Caller:** Hey [first name], I've got good news and bad news. Which one do you want first?

If they ask **"Who is this?"**:

> **Caller:** This is [your name] with Stellar Roofing & Restorations. I've got good news and bad news. Which one do you want first?

Then stop and let them answer. Their answer is permission to keep going.

> **Caller:** The bad news is a stranger is calling you out of the blue. I know that's annoying.
> The good news is I'm calling for a good reason. Give me ten seconds and I'll explain.

## 2. Why you're calling

> **Caller:** We've been replacing a lot of roofs around [their town] lately. A lot of them are getting older and starting to break down, which is when leaks start, especially going into winter.
>
> So all I'm doing is setting up free roof inspections and free estimates, just so you know where your roof stands. I don't know if you've started looking or not, but with a roof it's always better to be proactive than reactive.

**Only say "we've been replacing roofs around [town]" for a town where that's true.** If Stellar hasn't worked there, say "around Middle Tennessee" instead.

## 3. Qualify (ask all of these)

1. "How old is the roof, roughly?"
2. "Have you noticed any leaks, missing shingles, or stains on the ceiling?"
3. "Have you been thinking about replacing it, or getting quotes? How soon?"
4. "Are you the homeowner?"
5. "Is there anyone else who'd be part of the decision? Can they be there too?"

**Book it if:** they own the home, and either the roof is old or damaged, or they're already looking or open to it.
**Don't book it if:** they rent; the roof is under about 10 years old with no problems and no interest; or they only want a phone price.

## 4. Book the appointment

> **Caller:** Here's what I'd suggest. Nate, our owner, can come out, get on the roof, take photos, and show you exactly what he finds. It takes about 15 minutes, and you'll have a written estimate either way, no pressure. I've got [day] at [time] or [day] at [time]. Which works better?

Then confirm:
- Street address and ZIP
- Best phone number and email
- That every decision-maker will be home

> **Caller:** You'll get a text to confirm. Just reply to it so we know you're still good.

**Book every appointment on the calendar in GoHighLevel, even if Nate already said yes in chat.** If it isn't on the calendar, Nate doesn't see it.

## 5. Common pushback

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I'm not interested." | "Totally fair. Is that because the roof's newer, or you've already had it looked at?" (If newer or already handled, thank them and end the call.) |
| "How much is a roof?" | "It depends on size and pitch, which is why Nate comes out. You'll get a written estimate on the spot, and it's free." |
| "I already have a roofer." | "Good. A second written estimate costs nothing, and we'll match a comparable written quote." |
| "Just send me something." | "I can, but a price without looking at the roof is a guess. It's 15 minutes. Would [day] or [day] be easier?" |
| "Is this a sales call?" | "It's a call to offer a free inspection. If you don't need one, I'll let you go." |

## Alternate opener (Nate's other door pitch)

> **Caller:** Hey [first name], this is [your name] with Stellar Roofing & Restorations. Has anyone reached out to you yet about the roof replacements we've been doing in your area?

Then go to step 2. Same rule applies: only use it for an area where Stellar has actually been replacing roofs.

---

## Rules for every caller

- **Only say true things.** Stick to what we can back up: free inspections and written estimates, lifetime workmanship warranty on replacements, licensed and insured, in business since 2020, and based in Goodlettsville. Never invent storms, neighbors' names, discounts, or deadlines.
- **No storm or insurance promises.** Don't say insurance will pay. It's fine to say we photograph damage and can meet the adjuster on-site.
- **Retail offers only.** Free gutters and the Dog Oasis day only apply to retail (non-insurance) replacements. Don't use them on storm or insurance calls.
- **Do Not Call lists.** Respect anyone on the Do Not Call list and anyone who asks not to be called again. Mark them in the CRM right away.
