# 10 hooks for Nate to record (FB page and ads)

How to record: phone held upright, 15 to 30 seconds, one take, standing on a job site, at a truck, or on a roof. Say the hook first, then 2 or 3 plain sentences. Talk like you would to a neighbor.

1. "Got three roof quotes and none of them match? Here's how to compare them."
   Say: ask for each quote in writing, line by line. Ours comes with a free inspection and a written estimate.

2. "I worked for big roofing companies for years. Here's what I kept and what I left behind."
   Say: I took the good and none of the bad, so I started Stellar. Family owned.

3. "Roofing is loud. Your dog hates it. So we take them to a spa day."
   Say: the Stellar Puppy Promise. We pick up your dog on install day and take them to Dog Oasis. (Retail replacements only, not insurance jobs.)

4. "Repair or replace? Here's how you can tell."
   Say: look for a few signs, but the only real answer is an inspection. Ours is free.

5. "Why you should never accept a roof price over the phone."
   Say: nobody has seen your roof yet. We inspect first, then give you a written estimate.

6. "What does a lifetime workmanship warranty actually mean?"
   Say: explain in your own words what it covers on a replacement. Nate: use the real terms, I will not guess them.

7. "Storm just hit? Here's what to do before you call anyone."
   Say: take photos from the ground, don't climb up, call us. We photograph the damage and can meet your adjuster on site. (Storm and insurance audience only. Don't promise any insurance outcome.)

8. "Why is my company called Stellar? Out of this world roofing."
   Say: the story of the name and the astronaut logo, and that you want every roof to be a stellar job.

9. "Roof leaking at 2 in the morning? Watch this."
   Say: you can call any time, our phone is answered 24/7. Put a bucket down, move things, call us.

10. "3 questions to ask any roofer before you sign."
    Say: Are you licensed and insured? Is the estimate in writing? Is the warranty in writing? Ours is yes to all three.

Rules: no prices, no "best" or "most trusted", no Owens Corning or GAF names unless the disclaimer is on screen, and no promises about insurance. Nate says "since 2020" if he mentions how long Stellar has been around.
