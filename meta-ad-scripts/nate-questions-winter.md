# 10 homeowner questions for Nate (winter 2026 batch)

Nate answers each one in his own words on his phone, 20 to 45 seconds, one take. The answers are his: I do not know his answers, so I wrote no answers and no numbers. If he says a fact he is unsure of, cut it.

## Is my roof okay? (anytime, but good before winter)
1. How do I know if I need a new roof or just a repair?
2. What can I check from the ground, without climbing up, to see if my roof is in trouble?
3. How long does a roof usually last here in Middle Tennessee, and what shortens it?

## Getting ready for winter
4. What should I do to get my roof ready before the cold weather and the first freeze?
5. Why do gutters matter so much in winter, and what should I do with mine this fall?
6. Do tree limbs over my roof matter in winter? What should I trim, and when?

## Snow and ice
7. Is snow or ice on my roof a problem? Should I ever try to knock it off myself?
8. What is an ice dam, and do we get them in Nashville? How do I know if I have one?
9. I found a leak or a water stain on my ceiling after a snow or ice storm. What do I do first, and when do I call a roofer?

## Hiring
10. Can a roof be replaced or repaired in winter, or should I wait until spring?

## Notes
- Safety: for 7 and 9, Nate should tell people to stay off the roof and out of icy ladders.
- Keep Puppy Promise, free gutters, and any storm or insurance promises out of these (they are separate offers).
- Do not claim Stellar fixes leaks "same day" or responds "within X hours." Only confirmed: free inspection, written estimate, phone answered 24/7.
- Good ending for any answer: "If you're not sure, we'll inspect it for free."
