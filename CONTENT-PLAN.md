# Content Plan: What Stellar Can Realistically Rank For

Keyword research run 2026-09-28 with OpenSEO. It covered the keywords three competitors rank top 20 for (Don Kennedy, Bill Ragan, Southern Roofing Co.) plus metrics for about 150 city, service, and topic keywords. Cost: ~115 credits. Search Console had no data yet on the new Domain property, so re-run that part in late October.

Volumes are US monthly searches. KD is keyword difficulty, 0–100; lower is easier. For a new site with almost no links, **KD 0–20 with local or buying intent** is the realistic target.

## The opportunity

Nashville's head terms ("roofers nashville", 1,900/mo) go to sites with years of links. Three areas are winnable now:

1. **City pages.** Don Kennedy's top-10 rankings are mostly city pages, and most of those keywords are KD 0–21.
2. **Service keywords with KD 0**, especially commercial roofing, storm/hail damage, and emergency/leak repair. These have high CPCs ($20–$50), which means they bring real jobs.
3. **"Roofing basics" articles.** Bill Ragan ranks for 2,000+ keywords this way. Many are KD 0–8 with thousands of searches, including the two shingle lines Stellar actually installs.

## Won by Maps, not pages

"roofers near me" (165,000), "roof repair near me" (90,500), and "best roofing company near me" (8,100) go to the Google map box. The website helps, but **reviews, listings, and the Business Profile** decide these. See LOCAL-SEO-AUDIT.md and LINK-PROSPECTS.md.

## Skip

- **CertainTeed** terms: Bill Ragan's biggest traffic source (14,800+), but Stellar doesn't install CertainTeed.
- **Nashville head terms** ("roofing company nashville", KD 30–37): keep the `/nashville` page optimized, but don't expect page 1 until the site has links.
- Madison, Goodlettsville, Old Hickory, Whites Creek, Joelton, White House: no measurable search volume (except Goodlettsville, for the Maps reason above).

## Risks and caveats

- **KD can understate difficulty.** Product-name results (Owens Corning Duration, GAF HDZ) are dominated by the manufacturers and big retailers. A roofer's review post can still rank #5–10; Bill Ragan does, at #9–18.
- **Local volume vs. national.** City keyword volumes are real local demand. The "basics" volumes are national, and Nashville is a small share.
- **Close variants** ("roofers murfreesboro tn" vs "murfreesboro roofers") share one search-volume bucket, so don't add them up.
- **Search Console:** no data yet on the new Domain property. Re-run in late October to find near-miss queries.

## Recommended build order

1. **Weeks 1–2:** Hendersonville, Murfreesboro, and Franklin city pages; commercial roofing page upgrade.
2. **Weeks 3–4:** Clarksville and Gallatin city pages; storm damage and emergency page upgrades; the Goodlettsville page.
3. **Weeks 5–6:** Blog posts on Owens Corning Duration, GAF Timberline HDZ, and Owens Corning vs. GAF; the metal roof cost update.
4. **Weeks 7–8:** Roof inspection cost, roof leaking, and Class 4 posts; flashing section on the repair page.
5. **Ongoing:** the roofing-basics cluster, 1–2 posts a week.
